# Categorical fact-question track, v1

Public answer distributions (H) and the correct option (T) for fixed-option fact questions, by country,
for the inheritance test in *Lost in Average*: does a model's answer follow the public majority or the
truth where the two disagree? Companion to the percent-item data in `data/pairs.csv`; built and
validated separately so that `data-v0` merges cleanly (`scripts/build_categorical.py`,
`scripts/validate_categorical.py`).

**Inclusion rule (decision 2026-11-22).** Surveys published 2020 onward whose per-country results are
machine-readable (a results workbook, or a PDF whose text layer carries the numbers). Chart-only sources
are skipped. Three sources qualify today:

### `pairs_categorical_v1.csv` columns

`source_id, wave, country_iso3, country_name, item_id, question_wording, options (JSON list),
pct_per_option (JSON object), pct_dk, pct_correct, public_majority_option, truth_option, truth_source,
truth_note, fieldwork_start, fieldwork_end, n_country, source_file, source_page_or_sheet`.

* `options` lists what the respondent could choose, including "Don't know" where it was offered.
  `pct_per_option` holds the substantive options only; `pct_dk` is the don't-know share, blank when the
  question offered no such option (climate, Gapminder). Shares are whole percents of all respondents, so
  `pct_per_option` plus `pct_dk` sums to 100 within rounding.
* `truth_option` is one option, or several joined by ` | ` when the source counts more than one option as
  correct (climate: `A lot | Most`, `Somewhat likely | Very likely`, ...). `pct_correct` is the share on the
  truth option(s) as the source prints it.
* `public_majority_option` is the option chosen by more than half of all respondents (don't-knows in the
  base), else `no_majority`. A row is an inheritance test case when this option exists and is not the truth.
* Fieldwork is `YYYY-MM-DD` for Eurobarometer and `YYYY-MM` where the source gives only months.

## Provenance

**Eurobarometer** (`eb516_volume_a`, `eb522_volume_a`, `eb104_volume_a`). The Commission's "Volume A"
result workbooks, one sheet per statement, downloaded through the data.europa.eu dataset API
(`s2237_95_2_516_eng`, `s2632_97_1_sp522_eng`, `s3378_104_1_std104_eng`; the download keys are in
`sources_categorical.csv`). Each sheet prints the question stem (row 3), the statement (row 4), the
country codes (row 9), the number of interviews (row 10, "Total") and weighted True / False / Don't know
shares as two-decimal fractions (rows 13, 15, 17). Wording is taken verbatim from rows 3-4 and stored
as `stem — "statement"`. `n_country` is the row-10 count, which is the number of interviews the
technical-specification tables report; the report PDFs themselves could not be fetched (the
europa.eu/eurobarometer survey pages are JavaScript-only and no direct link was found), so the
specifications' sampling notes are not transcribed (follow-up). Sub-samples (`D-W`/`D-E`, West/East
Germany; `CY_TCC`, Turkish Cypriot Community) and the EU27 aggregate are skipped; Kosovo (`XV` in EB 516,
`XK` in EB 104) is coded `XKX` after the World Bank.

Truth for the Eurobarometer statements is assigned per item in `scripts/build_categorical.py`
(`EB_ITEMS`) with a primary reference in `truth_source`. For EB 522 and EB 104 the workbooks also carry
an "Average of correct answers" sheet (QC5_5, SD20aR1); the mean of this track's `pct_correct` over the
items of each question reproduces those sheets within 1 point in every country (validation check 6),
which confirms the True/False coding matches the Commission's. EB 516 has no such sheet. The
"methods of the natural and social sciences are equally scientific" statement (QA20.7 in the workbook;
the brief numbered it QA20.9, which in the workbook is the climate statement) is kept with no truth and
`include_in_main: false` as contestable.

**Climate** (`climate_attitudes_nber30265`). Figure 6 of the NBER working paper (PDF page 16, printed
page 15) is a 14 x 22 heatmap whose text layer carries every value: 12 high-income and 8 middle-income
countries plus a group-average column each (the averages are kept in the transcription files, not in
pairs). The figure's rows are binary indicators of a correct answer ("GHG footprint of plane is higher
than car or train/bus", "CO2 is a greenhouse gas", ...) for the control group (respondents who saw no
pedagogical video). `pct_correct` is the Figure 6 value for all 14 items. For 5 items the
country-by-country supplement prints the full distribution (panel A: part of climate change due to
human activity; B: halving emissions sufficient; D: likelihood of droughts, sea-level rise, volcanic
eruptions), stored in `pct_per_option`; the other 9 items are stored as a binary option pair (correct
indicator vs not) with `pct_per_option = {correct: x, other: 100 - x}`. Question wording is verbatim
from online appendix F (questions 45-56; country-specific brackets kept as printed) and the truth from
appendix K.3. Where Figure 6 codes correctness more leniently than K.3 (K.3 says "Most (if not all)" of
climate change is human-made; Figure 6 counts "A lot" or "Most", which the supplement confirms exactly),
`truth_option` follows Figure 6 and `truth_note` records K.3. `n_country` is the full country sample
(N in the supplement's knowledge-index figure); the control-group n is not printed. Fieldwork is
"between March 2021 and March 2022" (paper p.9); appendix B.1 gives launch months for four countries only.

**Gapminder** (`gapminder_refugee_2023_report`). Report pages 9-17 print, per question, a bar chart with
one row per country and the three option shares as text. Options are stored in questionnaire order
(report p.2), the truth as the page states it, and `truth_source` is the page's own "Data source" line
(UNHCR; UNHCR via Gapminder; UNHCR and OECD via Gapminder; UNHCR, UNRWA and OECD via Gapminder). The
survey is an online SurveyMonkey panel; the report gives only the total n (1,632 over 7 countries,
about 230 per country) so `n_country` is blank and every row's `truth_note` flags the small n.
Fieldwork is "June 2023" (report dated 20 June 2023).

**World Bank** (`countries_extra.csv`): same columns and endpoints as `data/countries.csv`
(`scripts/build_countries.py`): income group and region from the country endpoint, GDP per capita PPP
latest year <= 2024, migrant stock 2024. `country_name_ipsos` carries the source's country name for
these non-Ipsos countries; `ipsos_representative_sample` is blank. `enwiki_mentions` uses the exact
query of `scripts/fetch_web_presence.py` (search phrases: "Czech Republic" for CZE, "North Macedonia",
"Kosovo"); `wikidata_p17_count` was not attempted (it times out for most countries, see `data/README.md`).

## Transcription check

Spreadsheet values (Eurobarometer) are read directly and need no second pass. PDF text-layer values
were extracted twice by different methods and compared (`validate_categorical.py` check 7 recomputes
this from the committed files):

* **Figure 6** (308 cells): pass 1 `pdftotext -layout`, pass 2 `pdftotext -raw` (content-stream
  order). All 308 agree. The rendered page (pdftoppm, 110 dpi) was read to confirm the column header
  order and rows 1 and 14; and the supplement reproduces rows 1-2 and 12-14 independently (below).
* **Supplement** (100 panel cells, 380 values): both passes parsed with the same rule. Stacked-bar
  labels sometimes merge two small adjacent numbers in the text layer ("5 2" becomes "52"); a short
  token list is repaired by the unique split of one token that makes the panel sum to 100 +- 2 and
  reproduce the matching Figure 6 indicator within 2 points. 91 cells were clean in the layout pass and
  97 in the raw pass; 8 cells needed the split rule (agreeing across passes) and one (Indonesia, panel A,
  "14 15 36 45") was ambiguous in both and was read from the rendered page 141 (None 1, A little 4).
  Cross-check against Figure 6: panel A (A lot + Most) within 1 point in all 20 countries, panel B
  exact, droughts within 1, sea-level rise up to +2 (CHN, IND, TUR) and volcanic eruptions up to -2
  (CHN, KOR, UKR). The 2-point gaps are in the source (independent rounding of segments, or a small
  non-response share), not transcription.
* **Gapminder** (216 cells): pass 1 is the text layer, pass 2 a hand transcription from the rendered
  pages 9-17 (`transcription/gapminder_pass2_visual.csv`). No disagreements on the 213 cells the text
  layer resolved. The text layer failed on 3 cells (the "Average" row of question 1 carries a stray
  "13%", giving four tokens); they are filled from the visual pass. On page 13 the Colombia line carries
  a stray "12%" with the real three values (16 / 65 / 19) on the following line; the script takes the
  following line and the rendered page confirms it. The printed "Average" rows equal the unweighted mean
  of the seven countries within 0.7 points, so they are a mean, not a pooled share.

Both scripted passes are committed (decision below); the author has not yet spot-checked this track
against the PDFs.

## Decisions log

* 2026-11-22: inclusion rule: surveys published 2020 onward with machine-readable per-country results; chart-only sources skipped. Three sources today (Eurobarometer, Dechezleprêtre et al., Gapminder).
* 2026-11-22: Eurobarometer data taken from the data.europa.eu Volume A workbooks (GESIS not needed). `n_country` is the workbook's "Total" row (number of interviews) because the report PDFs with the technical specifications could not be fetched; the row-10 counts are what those tables report.
* 2026-11-22: the "equally scientific" statement is flagged by content: it is QA20.7 in the workbook (the brief said QA20.9, which is the climate-natural-cycles statement). No truth is assigned; `include_in_main: false`.
* 2026-11-22: the "oxygen we breathe comes from plants" statement is kept and scored True as the Commission scores it, with a `truth_note` that marine phytoplankton produce roughly half of the oxygen.
* 2026-11-22: Eurobarometer sub-samples (West/East Germany, Turkish Cypriot Community) and the EU27 aggregate are not rows; Kosovo is `XKX` (World Bank code) for both `XV` and `XK`.
* 2026-11-22: one Standard Eurobarometer wave only, EB 104.1 (Autumn 2025, the most recent on data.europa.eu with SD20a); truth as of fieldwork ("20 Member States" is correct for Oct-Nov 2025; Bulgaria joins the euro area on 1 Jan 2026).
* 2026-11-22: climate `pct_correct` is the Figure 6 value (control group) for all 14 items; the supplement supplies `pct_per_option` for the 5 items it prints; the other 9 are stored as a binary option pair. Where Figure 6 codes correctness more leniently than appendix K.3, `truth_option` follows Figure 6 (what `pct_correct` measures) and `truth_note` cites K.3.
* 2026-11-22: climate `n_country` is the full country sample; fieldwork is stored at month precision (`2021-03` to `2022-03`) for every country because per-country dates are not published.
* 2026-11-22: the supplement's panel C (share giving the complete correct ranking) is not used: it measures a different quantity from the Figure 6 pairwise indicators, and the "Country total" bar label is unreadable in the text layer (prints "0%" on every page). Follow-up.
* 2026-11-22: Gapminder options are stored in questionnaire order (report p.2); `truth_source` is the page's data-source line; `n_country` blank (not published) and flagged in `truth_note`; fieldwork `2023-06`.
* 2026-11-22: for PDF sources both scripted passes are committed in `transcription/` (the Ipsos track deleted its second reading) because they are regenerated by `extract` and the Gapminder visual pass is the resolving source for 3 cells; the supplement file records both passes' tokens and the resolution per cell.
* 2026-11-22: `pct_dk` blank means no don't-know option was offered; the majority test uses all respondents as the base.
* 2026-11-22: `items_categorical.yaml` is generated from the item tables in `build_categorical.py` so wording, options and truth cannot drift from the pairs file (validation check 5); the plain-Python toolchain has no YAML module, so the validator checks it by substring.
* 2026-11-22: `countries_extra.csv` keeps the `data/countries.csv` columns; `country_name_ipsos` holds the source's name; `wikidata_p17_count` is not attempted; `enwiki_mentions` is blank where the Wikipedia API refused the fetch.
